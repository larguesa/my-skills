# Sources, brand and attribution

## Example content

The example project is [T2S Tech](https://t2stech.com/). The five service pages below were retrieved on 2026-09-27. Each service has four examples, giving 20 styles without inventing 20 different offerings.

| Service | Verified basis for the examples |
|---|---|
| [AI-First Discovery](https://t2stech.com/services/ai-first-discovery/) | Problem map, testable value hypothesis, working prototype, system map and pilot plan. |
| [AI-Native Delivery Pods](https://t2stech.com/services/ai-native-delivery-pods/) | Experienced specialists and supervised agents; Prototyper, Builder, Sweeper, Grower and Maintainer roles; human review of decisions, releases and high-impact actions. |
| [Modernization and Integration](https://t2stech.com/services/modernization-and-integration/) | Incremental modernization, legacy decomposition, APIs/data, operational workflows, traceability and recovery-aware design. |
| [AI Quality and Reliability](https://t2stech.com/services/ai-quality-and-reliability/) | Testing, agent evaluation, security, observability and release evidence with human review. |
| [Managed Evolution](https://t2stech.com/services/managed-evolution/) | Recurring engineering for a prioritized backlog, reliability, improvement, integration and technical stewardship. |

Context, Delivery and Quality are shared principles on the service pages. Roles do not establish a fixed number of people. Diagrams, pipelines, terminals and instrument panels are explanatory illustrations, not screenshots of a T2S product or live customer metrics. No quantified performance gain is implied by a decorative graph, line, animation counter or visual progress.

## Brand assets

The official logo files came from:
- https://t2stech.com/assets/t2s-logo-dark.svg
- https://t2stech.com/assets/t2s-logo-light.svg

The examples preserve the exact SVGs and embed those bytes in `assets/brand.js` for safe local Canvas export. Primary visual accents are blue `#3264FF`, cyan `#00B4E8` and dark neutrals. Individual art directions introduce complementary palettes while preserving logo geometry and readable brand placement.

Montserrat and Rubik are distributed with their corresponding OFL notices in `assets/`. Preserve those notices when redistributing font files. The project uses font files for titles/body text, not an imitation of the logo.

This package contains newly authored instructions, scenes and tooling. It does not redistribute the reference repository's source, templates or private skill. An open-source license for that material was not established during the review; a public repository alone is not a reuse license.

`catalog.json` records the original style-directory labels for traceability. The reference label `kurzgesagt` is represented here by the generic **flat-vector explainer** category. The example uses original artwork and does not claim affiliation with or reproduce a studio's characters, assets or branded identity. All category names describe a visual language rather than endorsement.
