# Semantic typography: language changes the space

## Inputs
An original or cleared short statement, audience, intended reading order, two typographic roles, one operative verb and a transcript. Mark which words must remain readable and which can become temporary geometry.

## Direction
Give motion a linguistic job. To express connection, align separated phrases; to express hesitation, delay a resolution; to express release, open cramped spacing. Do not use every word as an excuse for a different effect. Example original line: "A question opens a route. A shared answer connects it."

## Storyboard and timing
- 0-3 s: establish the first complete phrase in a constrained but readable area.
- 3-6 s: the operative word changes the available space; retain a recognizable baseline or edge.
- 6-10 s: reveal the second phrase through that changed space.
- 10-13 s: resolve the relationship and pause without decorative drift.
- 13-15 s: hold the complete proposition or move to a distinct close.

Retiming follows phrase length and observed reading, not an arbitrary music grid.

## Motion and continuity
Measure loaded fonts before setting masks. Animate whole phrase groups first, individual glyphs only where meaning requires them. Structural letterforms may use outlines, but do not interpolate unrelated paths without compatible topology. Use an edge match or occlusion instead. Keep counters open and retain a legible text layer until the phrase has been read. Let a brief still hold make the resolution noticeable.

## Assets and rights
Write new language rather than paraphrasing a source speech line by line. Obtain quotation rights where needed. Preserve font embedding and redistribution permissions. Do not imitate a famous title sequence or attribute authored words to a real speaker.

## Output
Editable phrase cue sheet, original scene, text transcript and MP4. Narration is optional; provide captions when narration adds content not visible in type. The [kinetic style guide](../styles/kinetic-typography/SKILL.md) supplies a visual grammar, not a compulsory timeline.

## Pitfalls
Letter salad; premature word destruction; decorative strikes that reverse the sentence's meaning; tracking so tight that words merge; metaphors presented as measured facts.

## Validation
Read the encoded film muted without prior knowledge. Confirm phrase order and exact copy. Inspect masks around ascenders, descenders and counters at intermediate frames. Test small-screen readability and stillness after the final reveal. A fluent transcript cannot repair unreadable on-screen language.
