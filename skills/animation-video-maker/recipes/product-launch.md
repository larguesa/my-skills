# Product launch: problem, demonstration, transformation, proof, action

## When to use

An optional five-beat launch structure when a real product workflow and a supported outcome are available. This extends [product proof](product-proof.md) with a launch arc, not a new style or compulsory five-scene format. If evidence is missing, use an explicitly illustrative concept film instead of claiming a live demonstration.

## Inputs

Complete the [creative brief](../templates/creative-brief.md) and [visual direction card](../templates/LOOK.md). Bring an authorized current capture, product version/date, privacy-safe test data, exact approved claims, rights ledger and working CTA destination. Reuse product proof's evidence and capture safeguards. Never invent a customer metric to fill the proof slot.

## Editable sequence

| Beat | Visible job | Evidence and continuity |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Name one recognizable task or friction in a short sentence | Support the problem claim; avoid inflated pain statistics. |
| Real demo | Show the actual input, action and response at readable scale | Capture real software; disclose time compression and preserve necessary steps. |
| Transformation | Make the changed state legible through one continuous object or motivated cut | Preserve item identity. A decorative morph is explanation, not proof of a shipped interaction. |
| Proof | Hold the observed result beside the narrow claim it supports | Prefer the visible result; any number needs source, units, date and context. |
| CTA | Offer one truthful next action and hold the destination long enough to read | Verify availability and URL. A closing hold is the default, not a forced loop. |

Allocate time from reading and task complexity rather than equal fifths. Remove a secondary feature before speeding up the actual demonstration. Keep all scene boundaries and any authorized audio cues in one absolute-time table. Do not promise a musical drop or soundtrack when audio is not approved.

## Construction and checks

Use [semantic typography](semantic-typography.md) for a concise opening, [UI continuity](ui-continuity.md) for an explanatory transformation and [motion language](../techniques/motion-language.md) for the shared timing contract. A factual capture can remain visually quiet while an overlay directs attention. Do not redraw a fictional interface and present it as the real demo. Use [audio timing](../techniques/audio-cues.md) only with cleared, authorized sound.

Run the [minimum smoke test](../references/smoke-test.md), then review every beat and transition in the delivered encode. Confirm action precedes response, proof survives mobile-size viewing and the CTA does not replace the result before it can be understood. Deliver the claim/capture ledger, editable timeline, transcript, local assets and final MP4. This recipe is a planning template, not a newly rendered real-product launch example. [Inspiration and evidence limits](../references/creative-sources.md) apply.
