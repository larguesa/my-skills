# Product proof: show the action that supports the claim

## Inputs
One audience problem; one current, verifiable product workflow; access approved for capture; three evidence moments; exact copy; asset permissions; privacy-safe test data; destination and ratio. Record product version, capture date and source links. A feature mentioned in code is not necessarily available in the shipped interface.

## Direction
Prefer a single visible task over an exhaustive feature list. Keep the viewer oriented through a persistent selected item. Original example: find a document, add a review note, then show the note in the actual review queue. Capture the real action before designing decorative motion.

## Storyboard and timing
- 0-3 s: name the practical question alongside the starting product state.
- 3-8 s: show the actual selection or input at readable scale.
- 8-13 s: show the consequential change and its connection to the input.
- 13-17 s: hold the real result and a narrow, supported benefit.
- 17-20 s: give a truthful next step, or let the demonstrated result close.

Shorten by removing a feature, not deleting the evidence. For longer flows, disclose editorial time compression and retain any step necessary to understand behavior.

## Motion and continuity
Use crops and restrained reframing to guide attention. Cursor actions precede responses. Split screenshots into layers only when reconstructing verified appearance, label reenactments when they could be mistaken for operation, and never animate nonexistent interactions. Retain the same item identifier across shots. Keep annotations outside controls and preserve important warnings.

## Assets and rights
Obtain permission for the product UI, logos, customer content and people. Use a safe account, not live private records. Never reveal tokens, inboxes or customer identifiers. Synthetic test records must be labeled and cannot support performance statistics. Numbers need units, source, date and context. Capture assets locally under their actual permissions; public availability is not permission.

## Output
Original recordings, evidence/claim table, editorial timeline or scene, caption/transcript files and final MP4. Include an asset ledger and explicitly distinguish observed behavior from illustrative explanation. See [hybrid guidance](../backends/generative-and-hybrid.md) for media handoffs.

## Pitfalls
Invented metrics; redraws presented as evidence; speed ramps implying instant processing; benefit copy that exceeds the demonstrated action; cropping away limitations; decorative generated screens with false text.

## Validation
Compare every visible claim with its capture/source. Watch muted at mobile size. Verify action-result order and readable result holds. Inspect exact strings, icons, redactions and platform-safe margins in decoded frames. Verify actual video/audio streams and deliver the editable source, not only screenshots.
