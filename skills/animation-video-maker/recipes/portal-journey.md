# Portal journey: connected spaces, not random zooms

## Inputs
Three original environments or licensed plates; an opening in each that can plausibly contain the next; focal coordinates; depth layers; output format; loop decision. Sketch the destination composition as seen through each opening before animating.

## Direction
Make travel reveal a relationship. Example: a workshop window leads to a garden gate, whose opening reveals the workshop again. Keep one camera direction and a repeated material motif. Treat an apparent infinite zoom as a finite sequence of scale resets hidden by full-frame coverage.

## Storyboard and timing
- 0-3 s: establish the first space and its destination aperture.
- 3-6 s: approach, allowing the viewer to recognize the next space inside it.
- 6-9 s: pass the aperture and reveal the second space.
- 9-12 s: continue toward the next opening with consistent travel energy.
- 12-15 s: arrive at a conclusion, or occlude the reset into the opening composition.

Budget a longer segment for a larger scale ratio, not the same duration for every destination.

## Motion and continuity
For positive scales use `s(u) = s0 * exp(log(s1/s0) * u)`. Linear `u` gives constant log-scale speed; ease only the intended acceleration/deceleration zones. Place the focal point consistently in the composed camera transform. Match apparent scale velocity at boundaries. A portal must cover every output corner before switching coordinate systems. Define parallax layers explicitly, fill disoccluded regions and limit zoom to usable source resolution. For mathematical fractals, precision and sampling limits replace photographic resolution limits.

## Assets and rights
Use original geometry first. Cleared photographs, cutouts or generated plates can form a hybrid, with per-asset permission and provider records. Do not presume newspaper scans, vintage images or a generator's output are rights-free. A depth map does not reveal the real hidden background.

## Output
Scene/plate manifest, camera path, editable masks, transcript and MP4. Declare procedural 2D, true 3D or generated footage accurately. Generated clips do not guarantee repeatable object identity or a seamless portal.

## Pitfalls
Aperture edges exposed during reset; cropped plates at parallax extremes; destination jumping sideways; endless acceleration; soft artwork enlarged beyond its budget; nausea-inducing rotation.

## Validation
Inspect every boundary one frame before, at and after the reset, including corners. Compare focal location, scale and velocity across handoffs. Review the encoded film at normal speed, not only stills. For a return journey, validate both image and audio seams using the [loop recipe](seamless-loop.md).
