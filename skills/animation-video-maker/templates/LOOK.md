# Visual direction card

Copy this card into the production folder as `LOOK.md`. Complete it after the [creative brief](creative-brief.md), before authoring scenes. Reference the same card from every shot; change it deliberately rather than letting each scene invent a style. This is a visual contract, not another runtime or mandatory aesthetic.

- **Purpose and viewing size:** [message; audience; smallest intended display].
- **References:** [up to three cleared/public reference links; observable principles to study, not assets to copy].
- **Primary style:** [local style guide; what must remain recognizable].
- **Palette:** [background, text, accent hex values; contrast check; permitted exceptions].
- **Typography:** [licensed font files, weights, title/body sizes at export resolution; line lengths; one emphasis rule].
- **Layout:** [safe margins, alignment anchors, focal area, maximum simultaneous elements].
- **Material and depth:** [flat/tactile treatment; shadow, grain and blur limits].
- **Motion:** [one dominant device; easing/tempo; camera rule; preserved anchor; reading holds].
- **Evidence and assets:** [approved real UI/capture files; exact logos/copy; illustrative labels; link to asset ledger].
- **Avoid:** [specific unwanted defaults, invented controls/logos, competing motion, unreadable tiny type].
- **Continuity:** [what persists between shots; motivated cut or loop return].
- **Review frames:** [opening, hardest intermediate state, proof, closing; reviewer and accepted revision].

Approve representative stills at delivery size, then apply the [minimum smoke test](../references/smoke-test.md). A reference's popularity does not establish effectiveness. Use [motion construction](../techniques/motion-language.md) for implementation and [audio cues](../techniques/audio-cues.md) only when sound is authorized. Keep timings in one absolute-time cue table shared by picture and authorized audio; do not create a second competing clock.
