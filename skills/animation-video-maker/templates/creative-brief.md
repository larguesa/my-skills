Every interval must have a purpose. Build anticipation, action and resolution; vary pacing instead of adding random decorative movement. If looping, specify how the final motion continues into the first, not merely equal endpoint pictures. If not looping, protect the final message with a readable hold.

## Agent execution request

Using the completed brief, load the Animation Video Maker root and the selected local guides. Verify source claims and rights. Choose the simplest approved route that can preserve the required text, brand and timing. Build a contact sheet and short transition preview before the full export. Review actual imagery, correct defects, then render and decode the delivered file. Supply source, assets, transcript, technical metadata, reproducible commands and a truthful validation report.

For generative footage, first return the shot prompts and continuity sheet. Generate only within the approved budget and attempt limit. Composite exact text and logos deterministically afterwards. Label invented environments and illustrative interfaces. Do not claim a scene demonstrates real software unless it was captured from that software.

## Acceptance gates

- The visual action carries the message without the explanatory prompt.
- Copy, factual assertions, licenses and brand proportions match the approved inputs.
- Every shot and transition is checked in motion; sample frames alone are insufficient.
- Reading order, contrast, safe margins and transcript hold in the intended aspect ratio.
- The final file has the requested duration, dimensions, frame rate and audio state.
- Unverified providers, absent evidence and limitations are disclosed rather than filled with plausible claims.
